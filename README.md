## Content

### Widgets

Browser widgets for OBS to add more functionality to your stream.

- [All-Chat](https://allch.at) Free, open-source chat overlay that merges Twitch with YouTube, Kick, TikTok, and Discord into a single OBS browser source. Native 7TV/BTTV/FFZ emote support.
- [Ambiant Pixel Stream Packs](https://ambiantpixel-lab.github.io/obs-stream-background-pixel-art/) Animated pixel art scenes (Starting Soon, BRB, Ending, Just Chatting) with a live HTML browser source where you type your own title. Paid, $8 per theme.
- [Amuse](https://6klabs.com/amuse) Show information of the current song playing in your stream.
- [ChatIS](https://chatis.is2511.com/) jChat fork with 7TV integration.
- [CodingFreaks Chat Overlay](https://twitch-overlays.coding-freaks.com/en/overlays/chat-overlay) Free display-only chat overlay with native 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes and six themes. Runs from a single HTML file, so it needs no bot account and no OAuth token.
- [Facecam PNGTuber](https://www.facecam.ai/pngtuber-maker) Free PNGTuber maker in the browser that talks when you talk, with a one-link transparent OBS browser source. No sign-up.
- [Fut Overlay](https://futoverlay.com) Browser-source football (soccer) overlay for OBS with live scoreboards, lineups, stats and match events. Free trial available.
- [GGCombat Esports Overlay](https://ggcombat.com/en/widgets) Free 800x140 browser source for pro LoL, Dota 2, MLBB and AoV matches: both teams, live score or Bo format, and an Elo-based win-chance bar, reloading every 30 seconds. No account.
- [Kilobat CLI](https://github.com/seifip/kilobat-cli) Free, open-source macOS tool serving a local ping, jitter and packet-loss overlay for an OBS browser source.
- [Layrs](https://layrs.tv) Free hosted overlay editor for Twitch and Kick: chat with 7TV/BTTV/FFZ emotes and 7TV paints, alerts, goals and a subathon timer, edited live together with your mods. One URL in OBS.
- [OverlayCast](https://overlaycast.com) Browser-source overlays for OBS: chat, alerts, goal bars, timers, polls and chat games, each from one URL that updates when you edit it. Freemium: eight free widgets with a watermark, Pro for the rest.
- [PixelyChat](https://pixelychat.com) - Merges Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, and Kick chat, follows, subs, gifts, and likes into one browser-source overlay or dock for OBS/Streamlabs, plus neural TTS, live translation, and mod tools.
- [Poltergeist](https://poltergeist.cc) Real-time overlay control for Twitch, Kick & YouTube streamers and their mods.
- [send21 stream tips](https://github.com/send21io/send21-stream-tips) Self-hosted, open-source crypto tip page and OBS alert overlay. Viewers tip in USDC/USDT on Solana or BTC over Lightning, paid directly to the streamer's wallet, with an alert once the payment is confirmed.
- [Show Emote](https://show-emote.sammwy.com) Allow your viewers to display emotes from chat using !showemote.
- [showmy.chat](https://showmy.chat/) Instant, themed Twitch chat overlays.
- [SoundAlerts](https://soundalerts.com/) Allow your viewers to play sounds in your stream with bits.
- [StreamElements](https://streamelements.com/) Varied widget service (Chatbox, donation alert, goals)
- [Streamlabs](https://streamlabs.com/es-es/cloudbot) Varied widget service (Chatbox, donation alert, goals)
- [StreamTranslate](https://streamtranslate.live) Adds real-time translated subtitles to your stream through an browser sourse.
- [TipStreams](https://tipstreams.com/widget/) Free embeddable tip-QR overlay (OBS browser source): one QR for crypto, PayPal, Cash App, Venmo, and Ko-fi. No account, no fees.
- [Tramevia Dock](https://github.com/Tramevia/Tramevia-Dock) Free, open-source, self-hosted OBS docks and overlays for Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok: unified chat and event feed, one title/category editor for all channels, chat overlay, alerts and viewer counter.
- [Vizz.fm](https://vizz.fm) Browser-based music visualizer with customizable scenes and presets, usable as an OBS browser source.
- [Kloot](https://kloot.gg) Browser-based control deck for streamers: fire giveaways, live polls, lower thirds and hype effects from one OBS browser source, controlled from your phone.


### Analytics

Measure the data and analyze the traffic of your streams

- [TwitchTracker](https://twitchtracker.com/) Track Twitch channels, streams and subscribers along with global statistics.

### Chat Bots

Chat bot that brings utilities to your viewers or moderators.

- [ByteMate](https://bytemate.me/) Free all-in-one Twitch chat bot and web dashboard with commands, alerts, giveaways, OBS overlays and per-moderator permissions.
- [CoeBot](https://www.coebot.tv/) Chat bot with moderation, commands, quotes, variables and advanced integration.
- [Fossabot](https://fossabot.com/) The ultimate high performance, feature packed chat bot for Twitch.
- [Moobot](https://moo.bot/) Get a friendly, engaging, and loyal community on Twitch, all automatically, for free!
- [Nightbot](https://nightbot.tv/) Bot that allows you to automate your stream's chat with moderation and new features.
- [StreamElements](https://streamelements.com/) The chat bot with integration with StreamElements. 
- [Streamlabs](https://streamlabs.com/es-es/cloudbot) The chat bot with integration with Streamlabs. 

### Chat Clients

Alternative chat clients that bring more utilities or provide additional support.

- [Chatterino](https://chatterino.com/) A cross-platform chat client.
- [Chatterino7](https://github.com/SevenTV/chatterino7) A fork of Chatterino that support 7TV.
- [Chatty](https://chatty.github.io/) Java-based chat client for Twitch.
- [Composi Chat](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/composi-chat/mbbcmagdpiccgebggnkhpiledgodffbh) Twitch, Kick and TikTok live chat merged in one browser side panel, with 7TV/BTTV/FFZ emotes. Free Chrome and [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/composi-chat/) extension, no account.
- [CrossChat](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/crosschat-obs) Free, open-source desktop app that combines Twitch, YouTube, Kick, X, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and Rumble chat in one window, with replies to all platforms, moderation and chat/alert/viewer-count browser sources.
- [HeatSync](https://heatsync.org) Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat in one panel, with your own emotes in any channel.
- [Frosty](https://github.com/tommyxchow/frosty) A mobile Twitch client for iOS and Android with 7TV, BetterTTV (BTTV), and FrankerFaceZ (FFZ) support.

### Emote Tools

Tools and utilities about emotes.

- [Animated Emote Maker](https://animatedemotemaker.com) Turns a picture, or a character designed in the app, into a matching set of animated Twitch and Discord emotes, sub badges and PNGTuber states. 3 free credits, then paid credit packs.
- [Emote Tester](https://levi506.net/emote-tester/) App to allow for previewing Twitch and Discord emotes.
- [Twitch Emotes](https://twitchemotes.com/) It allows you to view the emotes of any channel.
- [Twitch Emote Resizer](https://tma02.github.io/twitch-emote-resizer/) Automatically resize emotes to required sizes.
- [EmoteResizer](https://emoteresizer.net) Free toolkit for streamers: resize emotes, sub/bit badges and stickers to exact Twitch, Discord, Kick, Slack, 7TV and YouTube sizes (animated GIFs supported), plus Twitch Bits and subscription revenue calculators. Browser-based, no signup.
- [Emote Resizer](https://emoteresize.com) Turns one image into every Twitch, Discord, 7TV/BTTV/FFZ and Slack emote, badge and sticker size. Supports PNG and animated GIF, batch input and auto-compression; runs fully client-side with no upload or signup.
- [EmoteStudio](https://emotestud.io/) Removes the background from any photograph and turns it into a clean emote for Twitch, Discord, Kick, Slack, and YouTube.

### Misc Tools

Various utilities for your stream that do not fall into a specific category

- [BetterStreams](https://betterstreams.tv) Stream automation that connects channel point redeems, subs, raids, and 16 other triggers to OBS actions, TTS, and giveaways. Includes reward management with auto-toggling by game category and redemption analytics.
- [Boltis](https://boltis.app) Free one-hotkey Twitch clip tool. Press a key while live to save the last 60 seconds, then edit to vertical with captions, cuts and overlays for TikTok and Shorts. You pick the moment rather than AI. Windows, no watermark.
- [BubbleFacts](https://bubblefacts.frolic.org) Free, open-source desktop app for music streamers (Mac, Windows, Linux) that shows fact bubbles in OBS about the song you're playing, from StreamerSongList or StreamElements requests.
- [chat.vote](https://chat.vote/) Create polls and raffles for the chat, let your viewers vote or make suggestions.
- [ClipMe](https://clipme.com) AI clip maker for live streams — turns Twitch, Kick and YouTube streams and VODs into captioned vertical shorts, ranking moments by chat activity.
- [Clips Kitty](https://colingpt9.github.io/clips-studio/) Free open-source clipper that runs on your own PC — turns Twitch, YouTube and Kick VODs into captioned vertical shorts, keeping whoever is speaking centred in the 9:16 crop. No uploads, no subscription, no watermark.
- [ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai) AI clip extractor — finds highlights in long-form VODs and renders short verticals for TikTok / Shorts / Reels.
- [Custom Spinner Wheel](https://customspinnerwheel.com) Free customizable spinner wheel for picking random names, splitting viewers into teams and drawing giveaway winners on stream. No account needed.
- [Gleem](https://gleem.gg) Creator page and Twitch extension where viewers can send paid messages, join memberships, unlock posts and request on-stream actions.
- [InstantClone](https://s1moscs.dev/instantclone) Free, open source stream delay for OBS that you can change while live, without restarting the stream: add a delay against stream snipers and drop it after the match. Runs on your own PC between OBS and Twitch, YouTube or Kick. Windows and Linux.
- [Link](https://github.com/shushu-eco/link) Free, open source stream recorder and clip maker that runs on your own computer. A browser extension records the Twitch or Kick stream in your tab with your highlights marked; a Windows program finds the moments and makes captioned 9:16 clips. Nothing is uploaded, no account.
- [NeonStreamLab Screen Makers](https://neonstreamlab.com/tools) Free browser-based tools to create Starting Soon, BRB, Ending and Offline screens as 1920×1080 PNGs. No account required.
- [NoSub](https://nosubapp.com) Watch Twitch and Kick VODs including sub-only replays, with the original chat replayed in sync. Free, no account, nothing to install.
- [PickerSpin](https://pickerspin.co) Free wheel spinner, dice roller and bingo caller for drawing giveaway winners — provably fair (Web Crypto API, publicly verifiable), no donation gate, no account.
- [RaidPort](https://raidport.tv) Twitch channel run as a raid gameshow for small streamers: raid in at the end of your stream, chat votes and a wheel picks a winner, and the channel raids them. Free, no login, nothing to install.
- [RandomWheel](https://randomwheel.com/streaming) Free spinning wheel for giveaways and decisions on stream. Add it to OBS as a transparent browser source, spin with Space, and optionally drop the winner after each spin. No account, no ads.
- [Remove Audio Music Remover](https://remove-audio.com/tools/remove-music-from-video) Removes background music from a clip while keeping the voice, so it can be posted without copyrighted songs. Runs in the browser on WebGPU without uploading; free for clips up to 15 minutes, no account or watermark.
- [ScaleReach](https://www.scalereach.ai) Turns an uploaded video or YouTube link into vertical 9:16 clips with AI captions, face-tracking crop and a virality score per clip, and schedules them to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Includes 100 free minutes with no credit card.
- [Streamable](https://streamable.run) Cloud-hosted OBS controlled from a browser for IRL streams: bring in a phone or backpack feed, switch scenes remotely and stay live through signal drops. Paid, from $39/mo.
- [StreamPulse](https://streampulse.fr) Free, open-source browser extension for viewers: one dashboard and desktop alerts for Twitch and Kick lives, auto-claimed channel points and Drops, and favorites pinned in the Twitch sidebar. No login and no OAuth token needed.
- [StreamWorlds](https://streamworlds.io/) A free Twitch extension that lets you create immersive 3D virtual spaces for your community. Boost engagement and fun.
- [Twitch → Telegram](https://github.com/Marfa/twitch-telegram-bot) Open-source Telegram bot for Twitch alerts (went live, category change, stream end, Drops), schedule tools and AI clips from VODs. Hosted bot or self-host.
- [Video Size Reducer](https://video-size-reducer.com) Compresses a clip or recording to a target file size (e.g. Discord's upload limit) and outputs an H.264 MP4 without watermark. Processed server-side; free up to 1 GiB, paid plans for more.
- [Voding](https://vod.ing) Browser-based Twitch VoD editor with chat-activity highlight finding, chat overlay rendering and FCPXML export.
- [Wheel of Item](https://wheelofitem.com) Free spin wheel & gachapon prize machine for streamers — viewers trigger it from chat with !spin, or it auto-spins on Streamlabs/StreamElements/Ko-fi donations.
- [Xenon](https://xenon-app.com) Dashboard for a spare screen, tablet or phone that controls OBS, Streamer.bot, Twitch and per-app audio, with a Stream Deck style touch grid and RGB lighting. Runs entirely on your machine, no account.

### OBS Plugins

Software to start streaming

- [Meta G Link Direct](https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/meta-g-link-direct) Uses Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) glasses as a live first-person camera source in OBS on Windows, over a Wi-Fi network the glasses host. Free and open source (beta).
- [OBS IRL Control](https://github.com/gleem-gg/obs-irl-control) Open-source OBS plugin for Linux that switches to your offline scene when an IRL stream drops or lags, and back when it recovers. No obs-websocket needed.
- [SELive](https://streamelements.com/selive) StreamElements integration for OBS.
- [StreamFX](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/streamfx-for-obs%C2%AE-studio.578/) Plugin that adds new sources, filters, effects and transitions to the OBS.
- [Tuna](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/tuna.843/) Show information of the current song playing in your stream.
